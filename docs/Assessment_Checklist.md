# Hospitality Cybersecurity Assessment Checklist

## 1. Network & POS Isolation
- [ ] Are POS terminals and payment devices placed on a dedicated VLAN?
- [ ] Is Guest Wi-Fi isolated from internal business infrastructure?
- [ ] Is Client Isolation enabled on the public guest wireless network?
- [ ] Are physical network ports in public/dining areas deactivated or logically isolated?

## 2. Authentication & Shift Management
- [ ] Does every employee have a distinct login account or PIN for POS access?
- [ ] Are manager-level privileges restricted and password-protected?
- [ ] Are default admin passwords changed on routers, switches, and POS hardware?

## 3. Data Protection & Physical Security
- [ ] Are POS transaction databases backed up daily to an offsite location?
- [ ] Is main network hardware (routers, switches, modems) secured in a locked rack?
- [ ] Are unattended POS terminals set to auto-lock after a short period of inactivity?
